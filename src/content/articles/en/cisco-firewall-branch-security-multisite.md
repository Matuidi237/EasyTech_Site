Organizations managing multiple locations face a challenge that traditional security architectures struggle to meet: how to effectively protect each branch, with limited on-site IT staff, while maintaining consistent visibility and security policies across every site.

For African companies operating across multiple countries or cities, this is a central question. A network of bank branches in Cameroon, a distribution chain in Ivory Coast, a telecom operator present in five countries: every site connected to the cloud or the internet is a potential attack surface.

## The branch is now the front line

Branches connect users, devices, cloud applications, and the internet. They often operate without dedicated on-site IT support, making centralized management and consistent security policies absolutely essential.

The Cisco Secure Firewall 200 Series brings enterprise-grade protection in a compact form factor designed for distributed deployments. It combines intelligent threat detection, native SD-WAN capabilities, encrypted traffic visibility, and centralized management.

## Four capabilities every modern branch needs

**1. Visibility into encrypted traffic**

Encryption protects legitimate communications but can also conceal threats. The Encrypted Visibility Engine (EVE) uses AI and machine learning to analyze TLS 1.3 traffic without requiring full decryption, preserving both performance and privacy.

**2. Protection against emerging attacks**

SnortML extends Cisco's Snort 3 intrusion prevention system with machine learning-based detection, capable of identifying unknown threats before traditional signatures are available.

**3. Secure, application-aware connectivity**

Integration with Cisco SD-WAN enables secure, intelligent connectivity between distributed sites. Zero-Touch Provisioning, reusable device templates, and dynamic path monitoring enable consistent deployments while optimizing application performance.

**4. Centralized visibility and control**

Cisco Cloud Control provides centralized policy management, visibility, and analytics across all distributed firewall deployments. Its multi-tenant capabilities also allow managed service providers (MSPs) to efficiently manage multiple customer environments from a single console.

## Two models for every environment

The **Secure Firewall 220** delivers up to 1.5 Gbps of throughput with NGFW capabilities enabled, in a compact form factor for smaller branches.

The **240P** provides higher capacity with additional interfaces and integrated PoE+ support for more demanding deployments.

## Relevant for Africa

In an African context where international bandwidth remains costly and connectivity can be unpredictable, SD-WAN's ability to dynamically monitor network paths and intelligently failover between links is particularly valuable. Centralized management from Cisco Cloud Control allows a central IT team to supervise dozens of branches across multiple countries without requiring on-site visits.
